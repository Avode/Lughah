FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need any Arabic background to start?

None at all. Lughah Foundation starts from zero — letters, sounds and your very first words. If you can already read, the goal quiz will point you to the right level.

Are classes taught in Malay or English?

Both. Our tutors explain concepts in whichever language the class understands best — most sessions flow naturally between Malay and English.

How do the live classes work?

You join a weekly small-group Zoom session at a scheduled time. Every session follows a structured lesson plan, and every student gets speaking time.

Is Lughah only for Quranic Arabic?

No. Quranic Meaning is one of our four tracks — the others focus on everyday conversation, absolute-beginner foundations and professional Arabic for your career.

How does the RM99 early bird seat work?

RM99 reserves your place in the beta intake. After payment, we contact you on WhatsApp for level placement and scheduling — and the RM99 is deducted in full from your programme fee.

What if I miss a class?

Class materials are shared after every session, and in a small group your tutor will help you catch up in the following class.

When do beta classes start?

The first beta cohorts are scheduled once early bird registration closes. Exact dates are confirmed with every student over WhatsApp.
