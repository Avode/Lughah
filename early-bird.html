Before you reserve

Things you might want to know.

What happens after I pay the RM99?

We contact you on WhatsApp within 1 working day to confirm, then run a short placement chat to match your level, schedule and tutor preference.

What if the beta intake for my track doesn’t open?

Your choice: your RM99 is refunded in full, or your reservation moves to the next intake or another track.

When do I pay the balance?

Only after placement, once your track, schedule and final fee are confirmed. Your RM99 is deducted in full from that amount.
